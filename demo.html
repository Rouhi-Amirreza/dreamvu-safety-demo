About these demonstrations

These are saved model outputs played over recorded footage, not a live camera feed. The models remain prototypes: missed detections, uncertain posture and identity changes can occur. Distance colors use apparent body size; no calibrated distance or Orin real-time performance is claimed. Event logging and feed health are integration views for the core platform.
